Crumple zones

Crumple zones stretch a crash out over more time. The same change in speed spread over longer means less force.

This isn't just a trick. Trucks are 20 to 30 times heavier than passenger cars, according to the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration. The collision force is identical on both, but that force has to change the speed of far less mass in the car. Which is the reason why the smaller vehicle takes the worse outcome. Keep your distance from heavy vehicles.
