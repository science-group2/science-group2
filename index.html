GROUP 2 - ROAD SAFETY

Every accident
obeys the
same rules of motion

Three interactive demonstrations showing how Newton's laws of motion explain what happens to a body in a car crash. Specifically, why seatbelts work, why loaded vehicles need more room to stop, and why the smaller vehicle always comes off worse.

Start with the first law
1 Inertia

THE LAW OF INERTIA

The car stops at the barrier. The passenger doesn't. Watch an unbelted dummy get thrown down the road, and find the belt strength that keeps them in the seat.
2 Force & mass

THE LAW OF ACCELERATION

Two cars, same brakes, same speed, and the same speed, but, one of them heavier. Hit the brakes and measure how much extra road the heavy one eats up before it stops.
3 Reaction

THE LAW OF INTERACTION

A car meets a truck. Both feel exactly the same force, the identical number appears on each side. Yet, only one of them is wrecked by it.

TEST — an interactive demonstration of Newton's three laws. Figures cited from the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration and the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration.
